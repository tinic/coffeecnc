# Milling research corpus

Collected from GitHub on 2026-11-14: **117 NC/NGC/GCODE/TAP programs, 29 STEP models, 7 native FreeCAD CAM documents, 2 CAMotics projects, 3 CSV tool/hole tables, 13 JSON task/feature specifications, a drawing and a setup sheet**, plus supporting documentation and upstream licenses across **11 collections** (199 upstream files, approximately 23.2 MB).

These are unmodified research fixtures. None has been executed in LinuxCNC, geometrically matched to its STEP file, collision checked, or qualified for a particular machine. Programs span LinuxCNC (`linuxcnc_post` and hand-written NGC), Masso, Tormach PathPilot/Mach3, Mach3/Mach4, Haas, Fanuc/CAMWorks, GRBL and Centroid/Avid dialects. They are useful precisely because real inputs vary in completeness, unit conventions, coordinate systems and revision consistency.

## Findings from inspecting the files

The [paired-job workflow review](../docs/research/paired-sample-review.md) examines Camcraft registration candidates and EngiWorld's native flip placements. It distinguishes initial stock from finished-part geometry and identifies interpreter gaps before either becomes an end-to-end demonstration.

### Kroneum: multi-revision enclosures, two-sided split ops, and implicit units

`kroneum` tracks the physical enclosure and PCB milling files for a hardware device across revisions `Rev_0.2` through `Rev_0.4`:
- `Rev_0.2` pairs [`kroneum.step`](upstream/kroneum/hw/enclosure/Rev_0.2/kroneum.step) with six operation- and component-split programs (`base_cutout.nc`, `base_inner-pocket.nc`, `base_outer-pocket.nc`, `button.nc`, `cap.nc`, `cap-without-pocket.nc`).
- `Rev_0.3` pairs [`kroneum.step`](upstream/kroneum/hw/enclosure/Rev_0.3/kroneum.step) with three consolidated multi-operation programs (`base.nc`, `button.nc`, `cap.nc`).
- `Rev_0.4` decomposes the job into 16 single-purpose files separating roughing, finishing, chamfering, cutout and two-sided setups (`base_base_clearing.nc`, `base_base_clearing_finish.nc`, `base_base_pocket_finish.nc`, `base_chamfer.nc`, `base_cutout.nc`, `base_slide_pocket.nc`, `button_i_base_*`, `button_i_top_*`, `button_x_base_*`, `button_x_top_*`), using `T6` (6 mm flat end mill), `T4` (1.5 mm fishtail end mill) and chamfer tools. However, `Rev_0.4` has no STEP file in its directory, demonstrating how shop repositories frequently store updated CAM programs beside earlier or later CAD exports.
- Every `kroneum` enclosure NC file starts with `G90`, `G0 G53 Z0.`, `T.. M6`, `G64`, `G54` and `G43 H..`, but **omits an explicit `G20`/`G21` unit modal code** even though tool comments and coordinates are in millimetres. A qualification check must flag missing `G20`/`G21` modals rather than silently inheriting machine state.

### Camcraft: exact Fusion 360 STEP-to-NC pairings with cross-unit STEP exports

`camcraft` provides script-generated Fusion 360 models and posted toolpaths (`masso.cps`) designed to compare CAM strategies against Fusion 360:
- [`ops_block.step`](upstream/camcraft/fixtures/step/ops_block.step) pairs with four fundamental operations (`ops_block_face.nc`, `ops_block_pocket.nc`, `ops_block_contour.nc`, `ops_block_drill.nc`).
- [`ops_2d.step`](upstream/camcraft/fixtures/step/ops_2d.step) (140 × 100 × 20 mm multi-feature block with through/blind holes, island pocket, open notch, slots, V-grooves and chamfered rim) pairs with 14 programs covering 2D adaptive (`ops_2d_adaptive.nc`, `ops_2d_adaptive_open.nc`), helical bore (`ops_2d_bore.nc`, `ops_2d_bore_passes.nc`), circular pocket, slot, chamfer (`lower`, `upper`, `width`), thread milling (`ops_2d_thread.nc`, `ops_2d_thread_cut.nc`) and trace (`ops_2d_trace.nc`).
- [`ops_3d.step`](upstream/camcraft/fixtures/step/ops_3d.step) (drafted pocket with filleted floor, corner radii and central dome) pairs with 3D ball-end-mill (`T2 D=6. CR=3.`) ramping toolpaths (`ops_3d_ramp*.nc`).
- [`ops_engrave.step`](upstream/camcraft/fixtures/step/ops_engrave.step) pairs with 90° V-bit engraving toolpaths (`ops_engrave_*.nc`).
- **Critical unit-scaling finding**: Fusion 360 exports `ops_block.step`, `ops_2d.step`, `ops_3d.step` and `ops_engrave.step` with `SI_UNIT(.CENTI.,.METRE.)` (centimetres), and `lego_4x2_01.step` with `CONVERSION_BASED_UNIT('inch', ...)`, whereas all paired `fixtures/nc/*.nc` programs run in `G21` millimetres. Any STEP loader that ignores the STEP file's unit context will mis-scale the solid by 10× or 25.4× against the toolpath.
- **Provenance filtering**: Upstream `fixtures/step/README.md` notes that three shop-jig files in the same folder (`sled_base.step`, `door_handle_guide.step`, `rail_guide.step`) were awaiting a public-release decision. CoffeeCNC deliberately excludes those three files and collects only the synthetic fixtures and golden post-processor outputs.

### EngiWorld CAM: explicit `linuxcnc_post` jobs, fixture avoidance, and `G54`/`G55` two-sided setups

The curated `engiworld-cam` tasks supply machine-readable task specifications (`task-*.json`), input STEP geometry, native FreeCAD 0.21.2 `.FCStd` documents and posted `.nc` programs:
- **Two-sided machining across two CAM systems (`task-17`)**:
  - In [`cli/freecad-path/task-17`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-17/), [`two_sided_part.step`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-17/init_file/two_sided_part.step) is paired with [`task-17_real.fcstd`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-17/ground_truth/task-17_real.fcstd), [`task-17_A.nc`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-17/ground_truth/task-17_A.nc) (`G54`, `T1` D8 flat end mill cutting the Side A through-pocket) and [`task-17_B.nc`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-17/ground_truth/task-17_B.nc) (`G55`, flipped work coordinate system, `T2` D6 drill with `G81` and `T3` D8 end mill for helical counterbores), posted with `linuxcnc_post` in `G21` mm.
  - In [`cli/solidcam/task-17`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/solidcam/task-17/), the parallel two-sided task has a metric [`tools.csv`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/solidcam/task-17/init_file/tools.csv), an inch-unit [`two_sided_part.step`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/solidcam/task-17/init_file/two_sided_part.step), and Fanuc/CAMWorks programs [`task-17_A.nc`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/solidcam/task-17/ground_truth/task-17_A.nc) (`G54`) and [`task-17_B.nc`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/solidcam/task-17/ground_truth/task-17_B.nc) (`G55`) posted in `G20` inches. `task-17_B.nc` invokes `G41 D23` for `T03 H03` (a Fanuc/Haas offset-number convention that does not match LinuxCNC's `G41 D<tool>` semantics).
- **Fixture & vise collision-avoidance geometry (`task-10`, `gui/task-11`)**: [`cli/freecad-path/task-10`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-10/) provides separate [`part.step`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-10/init_file/part.step) and [`fixture.step`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-10/init_file/fixture.step) solids with a boundary dressup maintaining 3 mm fixture clearance; [`gui/freecad-path/task-11`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/gui/freecad-path/task-11/) provides [`part_with_vise.step`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/gui/freecad-path/task-11/init_file/part_with_vise.step) with vise jaws in the STEP assembly.
- **Multi-tool, rest-machining, dogbone and holding-tab dressups**: [`task-04`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-04/) maps a 10-hole [`hole_table.csv`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-04/init_file/hole_table.csv) across `T1`/`T2`/`T3` drills; [`task-08`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-08/) combines `T1` D10 pocket roughing with `T2` D4 adaptive rest clearing; [`task-19`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-19/) coordinates five tools (`T1`–`T5`) from [`tool_candidates.csv`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/cli/freecad-path/task-19/init_file/tool_candidates.csv); [`gui/task-18`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/gui/freecad-path/task-18/) and [`gui/task-19`](upstream/engiworld-cam/task/single-software-execution/gui/freecad-path/task-19/) provide dogbone overcut slots and 6-tab cutout dressups.

### LongboardMarkII: imperial PathPilot/Mach3 programs paired with metric STEP models

`longboard-mark2` represents typical hobby/prosumer aluminum drivetrain fabrication (custom Caliber truck hanger, motor mounts, belt cap and battery holder):
- [`CustomHangerV2.TAP`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/Caliber%20trucks%20fixed/CustomHangerV2.TAP), [`MotorMountLeftV1.TAP`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/Motor%20Mount/MotorMountLeftV1.TAP) and [`MotorMountRightv2.TAP`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/Motor%20Mount/MotorMountRightv2.TAP) run in `G20` inches and call `M998` (Tormach PathPilot move-to-tool-change-position) before `T2 G43 H2 M6`.
- Their paired STEP models ([`CustomHangerV2_AP214.STEP`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/FullAssembly/LegacyFullAssembly/CustomHangerV2_AP214.STEP), [`MotorMountLeftV5_AP214.STEP`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/FullAssembly/LegacyFullAssembly/MotorMountLeftV5_AP214.STEP), [`MotorMountRightV5_AP214.STEP`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/FullAssembly/LegacyFullAssembly/MotorMountRightV5_AP214.STEP)) are exported in millimetres (`SI_UNIT(.MILLI.,.METRE.)`), and the motor-mount CAD is at revision `V5` while the `.TAP` files are `V1`/`v2`.
- The `.TAP` files use radius-format arcs (`G3 X... Y... R...`) and include degenerate zero-radius arcs such as `N580 G3 X4.5942 Y0.1 R0.` in `CustomHangerV2.TAP`.
- [`CNC/1.tap`](upstream/longboard-mark2/CAD/MarkII/CNC/1.tap) (`CONTOURED BATTERY HOLDER V6 DUAL`) selects `T2 M6` but commands `G43 Z0.6 H0` (tool 2 with length-offset index 0), a realistic operator/post hazard for qualification checks.

### LAIKA, FAB-2.0 and cam-occ: robotics & machine-build edge cases

- **LAIKA (`laika-cycloid`)**: Pairs [`Cycloid v1.5.STEP`](upstream/laika-cycloid/Actuator/Mechanics/Cycloidal%20Drive/Cycloid%20v1.5.STEP) with [`cycloid_1.nc`](upstream/laika-cycloid/Actuator/Mechanics/Cycloidal%20Drive/CAM/cycloid_1.nc) and [`cycloid_2.nc`](upstream/laika-cycloid/Actuator/Mechanics/Cycloidal%20Drive/CAM/cycloid_2.nc). Both programs issue `T1` without `M6` or `G43`, and `cycloid_1.nc` begins with `G28 G91 Z10` (an incremental +10 mm Z move prior to the G28 reference return).
- **FAB-2.0 (`fab2-cnc`)**: [`spindle support.tap`](upstream/fab2-cnc/Milling%20Files/spindle%20support.tap) switches into the ZX plane (`G18 G2 X41.656 Z14.5 I0.315 K0.`) for vertical arc lead-ins/lead-outs before restoring `G17`. [`4mm.NC`](upstream/fab2-cnc/Milling%20Files/4mm.NC) and [`F4_30mm.NC`](upstream/fab2-cnc/Milling%20Files/gcode/F4_30mm.NC) use Fanuc-style `:1248` headers, unspaced `N`-blocks, custom `M1010` macros and `T01 M6 M8` (multiple M-words in one block).
- **cam-occ (`cam-occ`)**: [`face.ngc`](upstream/cam-occ/trunk/models/face.ngc) assigns `#101 = 67.168904 (Safe height for rapids)`, rapids to `G00 Z#101`, emits `(MSG, test message)`, uses `G92.2` and annotates 3D surface cuts with curve-parameter comments. [`m-arcspiral-s-incorrect.ngc`](upstream/cam-occ/trunk/ngc-in/m-arcspiral-s-incorrect.ngc) provides an intentionally invalid arc fixture.

### Trebuchet: related files are not necessarily a synchronized job package

The upstream README describes a 620 × 300 × 15 mm MDF layout for a competition project. The setup sheet identifies the left CAM document, but its material field says `AusteniticStainlessSteel`. It also reports that the job has not been post-processed. Its May 11 save date precedes the May 15 timestamps in the collected NC headers. Treat the sheet as associated background with stale/conflicting fields, not an authoritative run checklist.

Both native CAM documents select `mach3_mach4`; the NC headers agree. NC uses millimetres, G54, M6 and G43. The left programs call T3; the right programs call T2, despite both tool-controller descriptions referring to a 6 mm end mill. Tool identity belongs to the setup and program revision, not just the visible cutter name.

Static inspection of the FCStd ZIP's `Document.xml` reveals rotated model clones: 90 degrees about Z in both jobs, with additional translation in the right job. The imported source object also has its own placement. A naive STEP overlay at the NC origin would therefore be unjustified. We have not composed and verified all placements or compared resulting solids/toolpaths. “Left” and “right” are upstream job/layout labels; we have not established that these mean a part flip.

The left native document lists 14 operation-group members while the selected `gcode-left` directory has 10 files. There is no established one-file-per-operation correspondence. Some posted paths also contain extremely large arc-center values; retain the original data for preview robustness research rather than normalizing it away.

### CAMotics: a project supplies context, but has its own semantics

The heart project explicitly lists `heart.ngc` and `cutout.ngc`, describes metric stock and defines tools in imperial units. Its NC files contain T words without M6 commands; CoffeeCNC cannot interpret a simulator's tool selection convention as proof that LinuxCNC changed the physical tool.

The wave project references `wave.tpl`. The separately stored `wave.gcode` is useful as a posted-path fixture, but regeneration equivalence was not checked. The current generator's scale is 2 while early posted X steps advance by 4; do not assume the two represent an identical revision. The generator has not been executed.

### LinuxCNC: a program is more than explicit XYZ line segments

The hole-circle example uses G20, G91, G81, polar increments and a repeat count. Grid probing uses named O-word control flow, expressions, G38.2 and probe-result output. The two-tool example uses G41/G43, M6, coolant and an M1 optional stop. Preview and execution state need interpreter semantics and machine context, not a regex that joins coordinate words.

### SM-101: useful geometry, deliberately lower evidence confidence

The source describes an AI demonstration built in October 2026. Its STEP, drawing and feature JSON are associated with a sensor-mount plate. The collected NC has readable operation comments and millimetre/G54 setup, and preselects tools ahead of subsequent changes.

There is a concrete mismatch: `tools.json` describes T4 as a 10 mm spot drill; the NC header describes T4 as a 5 mm flat end mill. The files can exercise revision/conflict handling, but must not be combined into a trusted tool table automatically. Claims of verification and time savings in the upstream README are the author's claims, not findings of this research.

## Remaining collection gaps

While the corpus now covers two-sided (`G54`/`G55` and top/base) setups, fixture/vise check geometry, multi-tool 2.5D/3D/thread/chamfer/engrave operations, and multiple controller posts (`linuxcnc_post`, Masso, PathPilot/Mach3, Haas, Fanuc/CAMWorks, GRBL, Centroid), we still lack a single end-to-end shop package capturing a physical LinuxCNC machine configuration (`.ini`/`.hal`/`.tbl`), touch-off/probing log, and mid-program tool-break/restart recovery transcript from the same physical metal run.

Additional leads: [mohanevs/CNC-VMC-Jobs](https://github.com/mohanevs/CNC-VMC-Jobs) has machining programs and STL rather than the requested STEP pairing; no root license was found during inspection, so no files were copied. [OpenATC](https://github.com/sn3ro/OpenATC) has STEP hardware and LinuxCNC routines, but those routines control tool changing and should not be mistaken for machining programs for the STEP parts.
